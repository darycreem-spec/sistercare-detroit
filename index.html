01 / BREAST SELF-AWARENESS

Get familiar with what’s normal for you.

There is no single “right” way for breasts to look or feel. Notice changes that are new for you and share concerns with a healthcare professional.

01

Look

Notice changes in size, shape, skin, or nipple appearance in a mirror if comfortable.

02

Feel

Become familiar with your breasts during everyday activities or in whatever way works for your body.

03

Notice changes

Pay attention to a new lump, thickening, nipple discharge, skin dimpling, or a change that persists.

04

Reach out

Contact a healthcare professional about a new or concerning change. Do not wait for pain to appear.

Remember: This guide is for awareness, not a diagnostic self-exam. A normal-feeling breast does not rule out a problem, and a lump does not automatically mean cancer.

04 / MAKE THE CALL

Words you can use.

You do not need to know what the change means to ask for care.

“Hello, I’ve noticed a new change in my breast and would like to schedule an evaluation. Can you tell me the soonest available appointment? Do you offer help if I’m uninsured or worried about cost?”
Important: SisterCare Detroit is an early prototype for education and care navigation. It is not a healthcare provider, does not diagnose, and does not replace professional medical advice. AI responses can be incorrect. Do not use this app to decide whether a breast change is safe to ignore. Seek professional evaluation for a new or concerning change.
